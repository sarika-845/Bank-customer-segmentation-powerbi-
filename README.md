# Bank-customer-segmentation-powerbi-

## 📊 Project Overview 

This Power BI dashboard analyzes bank customer behavior, demographics, and transaction patterns to uncover insights about customer risk levels, profitability, and engagement. The project uses real-world segmentation logic to identify high-value customers, retention opportunities, and demographic trends.

## 🔍 Key Insights - 
**Customer Segmentation**: Customers are classified into segments — *Loyal*, *New*, *At Risk*, and *Lost* — based on transaction behavior and recency.
**Top 10 High-Risk Customers**: Identified top 10 customers with high revenue but high risk status, critical for retention strategy.
**Credit Score Analysis**: Synthetic credit scores assigned to customers for profiling and risk comparison. 
**Risk vs Revenue**: At Risk customers hold significant revenue, highlighting retention potential.
**Demographics Breakdown**: - Females represent 75.81% of the customer base. - Most common age group: 30–39 
*Top locations: Mumbai, Bangalore, New Delhi, Gurgaon
**Transaction Behavior**: - Highest transaction volume in August–October ,Peak activity on Saturdays and Sundays
**Profitability Metrics**: - Total Customer Revenue: ₹1.65 billion
Average Transaction Amount: ₹1.57K
Maximum Transaction Amount: ₹1.56 million
Average Customer Balance: ₹115.40K

## 📈 Dashboard Features - KPI cards for revenue, customer count, and risk indicators 
Segmentation visuals: bar charts, pie charts, treemaps 
 Filters for age group, gender, location, and transaction date 
 RFM scoring and ValueScore comparison across segments 

## 🛠 Tools Used 
**Power BI Desktop**
**Power Query** for data cleaning and transformation
**DAX** for calculated columns and measures
**Custom visuals** for segmentation and behavioral analysis
